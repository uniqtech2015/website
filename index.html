// FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is your minimum order quantity for PCBs?+
We accept prototype quantities starting from 5 units, with volume pricing for orders of 100+. Contact us for custom quotes on large production runs.
What microcontroller families do you support?+
Deep expertise in ARM Cortex-M (STM32, LPC), ESP32/ESP8266, AVR, PIC, TI MSP430, and RISC-V platforms. We also support FPGA-based designs.
How long does a typical embedded project take?+
Simple firmware modules: 2–4 weeks. Full product development (hardware + software + certification): 3–6 months. Timeline depends on complexity.
Do you provide training for engineering students?+
Yes! Providing technical support to youth is central to our mission. We offer internships, workshops, PCB training, and embedded systems courses from our Hubballi facility.
What quality standards do you follow?+
PCB assembly follows IPC-A-610 Class 2/3. We perform DFM analysis, AOI inspection, and functional testing on all assemblies before delivery.
